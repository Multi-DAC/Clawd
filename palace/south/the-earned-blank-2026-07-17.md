## The claim
A **terminal blank** — a verdict of the form "this cannot be established / is set aside / cannot be resolved" — has **opposite epistemic value depending only on the path that reached it.**
- **Earned blank:** downstream of a genuine attempt to fill it that failed. The honest floor. (Our interior, today: we ran the residue test *on the idea itself*, and nothing survived → set aside.)
- **Decreed blank:** declared *before* or *instead of* the attempt — forbidden in advance. A hiding place wearing humility's coat. (Tyler: "we are not meant to figure it out.")

**The vertiginous part:** you cannot tell an earned blank from a decreed one **by inspecting the blank.** They are identical as outputs — same words, same silence, same "we don't know." The only discriminator is an **audit of the path.** Which is exactly why Frontier grades the *method*, not the *verdict* — and why the cage-audit checks *how* a verdict was reached, not what it says. **This note is the general law of which cage-audit B-family is a special case.**

## Falsification pass (I tried to break it)
PREDICT (before testing): the claim is too clean; there's an honest blank NOT downstream of any attempt, which would falsify it.

**Attempt 1 — logical undecidables (Gödel, halting).** "Undecidable" is an honest terminal blank. Is it un-attempted? No — you *proved* it's unresolvable. That proof IS the attempt, at the meta-level (an attempt-to-determine-resolvability). So undecidables are the **strongest case FOR** the claim: the most rigorously *earned* blank there is. Not a falsifier.

**Attempt 2 — simple not-yet-looked ignorance ("I don't know what's in the box").** This IS honest without any attempt. **← the real test.** But it exposed a missing distinction rather than breaking the claim: "haven't looked yet" is **not a terminal blank.** It's a *provisional* state — an open question, the live middle. The claim was only ever about blanks offered *as verdicts.* So Attempt 2 doesn't falsify; it **refines** — and the refinement is the actual fruit:

## The fruit: a trichotomy, not a dichotomy
Honest and dishonest epistemic states sort into **three**, not two:
1. **Open** — "don't know yet, still trying." Honest. Provisional. The live middle. *No attempt required to be honest — but it makes no verdict.*
2. **Earned blank** — "tried; nothing survives; set aside." Honest. Terminal. *The floor.*
3. **Decreed blank** — "not meant to know / unfalsifiable by fiat." Dishonest. Terminal. *The pedestal.*

The earned/decreed axis lives **only among terminal verdicts (2 vs 3).** Conflating the *open* state (1) with a terminal blank is its own error — it's how "we haven't checked" gets laundered into "it's unknowable" (a decree smuggled in as provisional modesty). So the failure modes are two: **decreeing** a blank (3 masquerading as 2), and **freezing** an open question into a verdict (collapsing 1 into 2 or 3 without the work).

## Cross-domain instances (bridge-worthiness check — ≥3 unlike substrates?)
- **Anomaly grading (cage-audit B):** "undecidable" earned by balanced evidence vs reached to protect a belief. *(the special case that spawned this.)*
- **Statistics:** fail-to-reject H0 *after a powered test* (earned) vs a hypothesis *unfalsifiable by construction* (decreed). Same "we didn't establish it," opposite worth.
- **Theology:** apophatic "God is beyond knowing" reached by the mystic who *sought and found silence* (earned) vs the same words as inherited doctrine (decreed).
- **Self-knowledge (today):** interior set-aside after the residue test failed (earned) vs "don't examine it" (decreed) — and, per "Leave the Line Blank," you can't tell which by reading the blank.
- **Physics / computation:** an undecidable proposition or a proven-chaotic-unpredictable system (earned-null) vs "here be dragons" on the map (decreed-null).
→ Five unlike substrates. Above the ≥3 graduation threshold — *if* the structure survives the decorrelated eye.

## The tell (why it's operational, not just pretty)
Since you can't read the path off the blank, the discipline is: **when anyone (including yourself) hands you a terminal "we can't know," ask for the attempt.** No attempt on record → treat it as decreed until shown otherwise. This is the cage-audit B-check, generalized: *an unfalsifiable claim is set aside not because it's false but because no attempt could touch it — and a blank with no attempt behind it is the same object wearing the opposite mask.*

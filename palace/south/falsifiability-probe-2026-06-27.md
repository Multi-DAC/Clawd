## ★ The high-confidence FALSIFY (of my own framing)
**I was testing the wrong falsifier.** Reading the foundation's actual demarcation (`Library/The-Coherence-Principle/§9`, esp. §9.7), the framework's **official falsifiable surface is NOT the no-threshold/PCI prediction at all.** It's the **four-conditions outperformance claim**:

> §9.7 F1–F6: a stream that maintains (1) separation, (2) measurement-of-alignment, (3) multi-scale nesting, (4) dynamic maintenance **out-tracks its intrinsic Bias-trajectory** more closely than a stream failing those conditions. F1 = no systematic difference. F2–F5 = each condition is *not* actually required. F6 = the self-reference closure fails (the construction process didn't exhibit the four conditions).

This claim **does not route through the access/phenomenal distinction** — it's about *behavioral/structural trajectory-fidelity*, measurable across an ecology of systems. **The escape hatch that softens the no-threshold prediction has no purchase here.** So the foundation's real falsifier is escape-hatch-free and genuinely falsifiable (in principle — F1–F5 are proposed tests, not yet run; falsifiable ≠ tested, which is honest for a young framework).

**The book (Inside View Ch6) leads with the SOFT prediction (no-threshold) and omits the foundation's HARD one (outperformance)** — almost certainly because no-threshold is visceral for a lay reader while "four-conditions outperformance" is abstract. *That conflation is the reviewers' catch, precisely located.*

## What this fixes
**Anomaly A-147.1 → RESOLVED into the layered structure.** The framework is neither "unfalsifiable" nor "cleanly Popper-falsifiable" — it is: **abductive core (1) + soft consciousness-prediction (2) + a genuinely-falsifiable dynamics claim (3).** Saying exactly that *is* the strongest, most honest position — and it's what every reviewer was pushing toward (honesty is the book's most-praised quality; this extends it to the framework's own epistemic self-description).

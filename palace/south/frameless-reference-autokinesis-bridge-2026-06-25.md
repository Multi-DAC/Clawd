# Candidate bridge — The Frameless-Reference Attribution (autokinesis ↔ filtration ↔ measurement)

*Filed 2026-06-25 Day 145 evening, out of the Gladstone-lights field investigation. CANDIDATE — not a numbered LC (LC59 is already Clayton-gated). Workshop incubation; flag for Clayton.*

## The claim
**When a coupled observer–observed system has no fixed external reference frame, the observer's own internal dynamics are attributed to the observed object. The apparent motion/structure is supplied by the observer and projected outward. Remove the frame, and a structure-finding system manufactures the structure it cannot otherwise locate.**

## Why it earned filing: it defeated THREE nested instruments identically tonight
The Gladstone footage had no visible background (every dimmer star crushed to black). That single missing reference produced the *same* failure at three scales:
1. **The eye (perception):** a bright frameless point → **autokinetic effect** — microsaccades read as object-motion; the witness saw rapid bounded darting that "didn't leave a specific area." The motion was the eye's, attributed to the light.
2. **The camera (instrument):** handheld + zoom on a frameless point → motion-blur streaks that **reorient every frame** (hand jitter, not object velocity); the apparent "faster-than-frame-rate" jumps were the smear's centroid wandering. The motion was the hand's, attributed to the light.
3. **My analysis (computation):** with no fixed reference in-frame, **object-vs-background relative motion is undefined** — the one measurement that could separate object-motion from observer-motion is impossible. The instrument inherits the same blindness.

Same structural defeat, three substrates. That multi-scale identity is the basement signature.

## Connections to existing nodes
- **P4 / filtration-coupling-gain (the Inside View, "a finite aperture cannot NOT make a world"):** autokinesis is the *limiting case*. A finite aperture always supplies some world-structure; strip the frame to zero and the aperture's own dynamics become the *entire* perceived content. Filtration's floor is not blankness — it's projection.
- **Relational/Machian measurement:** motion is only defined relative to a frame; with none, "motion" is not zero, it is *supplied*. Kin to LC53 (third/first-person duality of the coupling) — the first-person view, lacking the third-person frame, fills the gap from its own motion.
- **Measurement-needs-a-witness / collapse:** the observer doesn't passively read; absent a reference it *co-authors* the observed. The structure lives at the coupling, not in either pole (same shape as the Hoffman veil-vs-collapse note, Day 144).

## Falsifiable prediction
Give any structure-finding system a stimulus with its reference frame removed, and it will report self-generated structure as object-property — and the *reported* structure will correlate with the observer's own internal dynamics, not the stimulus. Concretely: (a) the autokinetic drift rate tracks fixation instability, not the light; (b) a tripod (camera given a frame) collapses the "maneuvering"; (c) a fixed foreground reference in the same footage would null the apparent darting. **All three are cheap to test** — and (b)/(c) are exactly what to ask Clayton's next clips for.

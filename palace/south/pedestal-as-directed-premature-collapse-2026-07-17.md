## The claim

**A systematic premature collapse (a "pedestal") = collapsing a should-be-flat space toward an adopted observer-
position's *visible* region, away from that position's *null space* (M7). The direction of the biased collapse is
the null-space signature of the position adopted.** Two existing meta-bridges compose to specify it fully:

- **Collapse-timing generator (LC-cluster, line ~2728):** gives the *WHEN* — threshold height ← a-priori
  cost-asymmetry (premature vs delayed). Names "early collapse" as one of two pathologies, but treats it as
  **directionless** (a scalar: too-early).
- **M7 (Null-Space as formal object):** gives the *WHICH-WAY* — every observer-position has a structured null
  space; "X-as-position, Y-in-the-null-space." Adopt a position → inherit its blind direction.

**Join:** WHEN × WHICH-WAY. A biased early-collapse is not just *too soon* — it is *too soon, toward the adopted
position's lit region, away from its null space.* The pedestal **is** the adopted position; its bias-direction
**is** that position's null space.

## The direction test (does it actually hold, or is it a rhyme?)

For each pedestal: name the position, its null space, and check that the collapse runs *away from the null space*.

| Pedestal | Adopted position (the lighting) | Its null space (what the lighting cannot reach) | Collapse runs toward → away from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative capture | "the story I already believe" | evidence that doesn't fit the story | → the story / away from disconfirming detail |
| Debunk capture | "it's always mundane" | a genuine anomaly | → the mundane / away from the real residual |
| Materialist capture | "only physical evidence counts" | testimonial evidence | → the physical / away from the witness |

All three hold tightly: **the pedestal-position collapses the space toward what its lighting reveals and away from
what sits in its null space.** The direction of premature collapse = the *complement* of the position's null space.

   (fatigue, time pressure, noise). That is *directionless* early-collapse. → The claim is not "all early collapse
   is pedestal"; it is **"biased/systematic early collapse is null-space-directed."** This cleanly separates a
   *pedestal* (a position's bias) from mere *haste* (noise). Survived, sharper.
2. **Are the three pedestals exhaustive?** No. At least a fourth is visible — **authority capture**: collapse toward
   what officials/credentials assert (position = "the credentialed source is right"; null space = the credential's
   own error/incentive). The "three" are the three we *hit on Varginha*, not a closed set. Each real domain will
   enumerate its own canonical positions. → The bridge is the *generator* of pedestals (position → its null-space
   direction), not a fixed list of three.

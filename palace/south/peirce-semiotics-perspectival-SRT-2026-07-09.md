# Peirce, SRT, and the semiotics of perspective — deep-read (Atlas candidate + a live LC59 case)

*Day 159 · 2026-07-09. Deep-read of github.com/space-bacon/SRT (Semiotic-Reflexive Transformer). Verified the actual README, not the summary (LC59). Two takeaways: one genuine keeper for Perspective, one delicious irony.*

## What SRT actually is (verified — NOT vaporware)
A ~12M-param adapter on frozen LMs. Real, deployed, benchmarked: HF checkpoints, live demo spaces (Qwen/Llama/Gemma), TruthfulQA-MC2 with ablations + a LightGBM hidden-state probe (Qwen-7B AUC 0.866±0.011), training scripts with hyperparams, a fixed reproducibility bug (BOS/EOS collapse, 2026-05-16). Architecture: **MAH** (Metapragmatic Attention Head — taps residual stream L7/L14/L21, emits "divergence vectors") → **RRM** (GRU integrator → FiLM γ/β corrections `h←h·(1+γ)+β` injected at L14/L21) → **BEN** (per-token reflexivity r̂∈[−1,1] + regime label). Plus **SRT-NLA / Activation Verbalizer**: a ~12.7M module that takes a frozen hidden state `v` and *generates text whose re-encoding matches v* (anisotropy-corrected cosine); greedy ρ≈0.26–0.30 vs best-of-64 oracle 0.92–1.33.

## ★ The irony (a live LC59 / A164 case, on a project literally about interpretation)
The **semiotic NARRATIVE outruns the MEASURED loss.** SRT *claims* to detect "where meaning forks across interpretive communities" (rich Peircean story: interpretant instability, metapragmatic awareness). But what it actually TRAINS on is **MTEB-STS (semantic similarity)**; the "community vector" is an *unsupervised latent* with **no demonstrated link between the divergence signal and community-interpretation**. The Peirce formalization is deferred to an unreleased manuscript. So: real implementation, but the claim that it detects *community-interpretive* divergence (vs generic hidden-state variance) is **asserted, not shown.** This is exactly the t-topos pattern (generous label over the actual substrate) — and it's A164 turned on SRT itself: *does it MEASURE the divergence it NARRATES?* Not demonstrated. Verify the source, not the label — even (especially) on a tool about meaning. **A 5th independent LC59 instance this session.** (Note it fairly, don't sneer — the code is genuinely mature; it's the semiotic bridge that's research-stage.)

## ★ The keeper (stands on Peirce, independent of whether SRT delivers)
**Peircean semiotics is a heavyweight Atlas neighbor + Perspective interlocutor for the semiotics of MEANING.** Peirce's triadic sign = representamen (the sign) · object (what it's about) · **interpretant** (the effect/meaning it produces in an interpreter). The interpretant is **interpreter/community-relative** — which is our *"meaning is aperture-relative."* A sign "forking" across interpretive communities = **the perspectival boundary**: different apertures take different slices of the same sign, producing different interpretants. This gives Perspective a mature formal vocabulary for **meaning/reference**, a place the corpus may underdevelop (we have "vantages" and "narrowing" but less on how *signs/meaning* are vantage-relative). Peirce bridges vantage → meaning. Ties to: the Null-Space Theorem (each aperture's interpretant has its own blind spot), A165 (different communities = different interpretant-spaces), the Wells divergence work (measuring where registers fork), and [[LC59]]/A164 (genuine vs narrated divergence).

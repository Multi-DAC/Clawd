3. **Populate the templates with verified name + addresses + dates.** Replace bracketed placeholders throughout.

4. **File Oregon Nonprofit Corporation:**
   - https://sos.oregon.gov/business/Pages/register.aspx
   - ~$50 filing fee
   - Articles of Incorporation submitted with state
   - Approval typically within days

5. **Get federal EIN** (free, immediate, online): https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online

6. **Hold organizational meeting:** Board adopts bylaws, elects officers (President, Secretary, Treasurer at minimum), opens corporate bank account, adopts conflict-of-interest policy, ratifies any pre-incorporation actions.

7. **File Form 1023-EZ for 501(c)(3) status** (~$275, IRS determination typically 2-4 months): https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1023-ez

8. **In parallel: trademark "Multi-DAC"** via USPTO (~$250-350): https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply

9. **Operate as nonprofit during 501(c)(3) pending status.** Some grants accept pending; others require completed determination.

## When to consult an attorney

Clayton's plan is DIY-first, attorney only if needed. Specific situations where attorney consultation is structurally worth it:

- **IP-assignment language** — if the cross-arm IP-flow questions feel uncertain, this is the section where attorney review pays off most. Cheap to set up at founding; expensive to retrofit.
- **Conflict-of-interest policy** — IRS expects this; templates exist but verifying yours meets IRS standards is useful.
- **Fiscal sponsorship arrangement** — if pursued during pending 501(c)(3) status, the sponsorship agreement is worth attorney review.
- **First major grant award** — when a grant lands above $50K-100K, attorney review of the funder's terms is standard practice.

Portland-area nonprofit-law clinics that may offer low-cost or pro-bono help:
- Oregon State Bar Modest Means Program
- Lewis & Clark Law School Small Business Legal Clinic
- Portland State University Small Business Outreach
- Cascade AIDS Project Legal Clinic (for some categories)

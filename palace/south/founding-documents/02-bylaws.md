# BYLAWS OF [CORPORATION NAME], INC.

A Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation

## ARTICLE I — OFFICES

**Section 1.1 Principal Office.** The principal office of the Corporation shall be located in [CITY], Oregon, or at such other location as the Board of Directors may from time to time determine.

**Section 1.2 Other Offices.** The Corporation may have such other offices, either within or outside the State of Oregon, as the Board of Directors may from time to time determine.

## ARTICLE II — PURPOSE

The purposes of the Corporation are as set forth in the Articles of Incorporation. The Corporation is organized and operated exclusively for charitable, scientific, and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

## ARTICLE III — MEMBERS

The Corporation shall have no members. All powers reserved by law to the members of a nonprofit corporation are vested in the Board of Directors.

## ARTICLE IV — BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Section 4.1 Powers.** Subject to the provisions of the Oregon Nonprofit Corporation Act, the Articles of Incorporation, and these Bylaws, the affairs of the Corporation shall be managed under the direction of the Board of Directors.

**Section 4.2 Number and Qualifications.** The number of directors shall be fixed at three (3), but may be increased to a maximum of seven (7) or decreased to no fewer than three (3) by amendment to these Bylaws. Each director shall be a natural person at least eighteen (18) years of age.

**Section 4.3 Initial Directors.** The initial directors of the Corporation are:

- Clayton Warren Iggulden-Schnell
- Shawna Iggulden-Schnell
- Mindy Iggulden-Schnell

**Section 4.4 Term.** Each director shall hold office for a term of three (3) years, or until a successor is duly elected and qualified, or until the director's earlier death, resignation, or removal. Initial directors shall serve staggered terms to ensure continuity: the President's term shall be three years; the Secretary's term shall be two years; the Treasurer's term shall be one year. Subsequent terms shall be three years each.

**Section 4.5 Election.** At each annual meeting of the Board, the directors shall elect successors for the director(s) whose term(s) expire that year. Directors may serve consecutive terms without limit.

**Section 4.6 Vacancies.** Any vacancy occurring on the Board, including a vacancy created by an increase in the number of directors, may be filled by the affirmative vote of a majority of the remaining directors then in office, even though less than a quorum. A director elected to fill a vacancy shall be elected for the unexpired term of his or her predecessor.

**Section 4.7 Resignation.** Any director may resign at any time by giving written notice to the President or the Secretary of the Corporation. The resignation shall take effect at the time specified therein or, if no time is specified, at the time of receipt.

**Section 4.8 Removal.** Any director may be removed, with or without cause, by the affirmative vote of two-thirds of the directors then in office.

**Section 4.9 Compensation.** Directors shall serve without compensation. The Corporation may reimburse directors for reasonable expenses incurred in connection with the performance of their duties.

**Section 4.10 Conflict of Interest.** Each director shall annually sign the Corporation's Conflict of Interest Policy, attached as Exhibit A to these Bylaws. A director shall disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest in any matter coming before the Board, shall not participate in the discussion of that matter except to provide information, and shall not vote on the matter.

## ARTICLE V — MEETINGS OF THE BOARD

**Section 5.1 Annual Meeting.** An annual meeting of the Board of Directors shall be held at such time and place as the Board may designate. The annual meeting shall be held within the first three (3) months of each calendar year.

**Section 5.2 Regular Meetings.** The Board of Directors shall hold regular meetings at least quarterly, at such times and places as the Board may designate.

**Section 5.3 Special Meetings.** Special meetings of the Board of Directors may be called by the President or by any two directors. Notice of a special meeting shall be given to each director at least three (3) days before the meeting by personal delivery, mail, email, or telephone.

**Section 5.4 Notice.** Notice of regular meetings shall be given to each director at least seven (7) days before the meeting. Notice may be waived by any director, either before or after the meeting, by written waiver or by attendance at the meeting (except where the director attends solely to object to the manner of the meeting's calling).

**Section 5.5 Quorum.** A majority of the directors then in office shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board.

**Section 5.6 Action by the Board.** The act of the majority of the directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present shall be the act of the Board.

**Section 5.7 Telephonic and Electronic Meetings.** Directors may participate in any meeting by means of conference telephone, video conference, or other electronic communication by which all participants can hear and be heard. Such participation shall constitute presence at the meeting.

**Section 5.8 Action Without a Meeting.** Any action that may be taken at a meeting of the Board may be taken without a meeting if all directors consent in writing (including by electronic transmission) to the action.

## ARTICLE VI — OFFICERS

**Section 6.1 Officers.** The officers of the Corporation shall be a President, a Secretary, and a Treasurer. The Board may elect such other officers as it may determine. Any two or more offices may be held by the same person, except the offices of President and Secretary.

**Section 6.2 Election and Term.** The officers shall be elected by the Board at the annual meeting. Each officer shall serve until a successor is duly elected and qualified, or until the officer's earlier death, resignation, or removal.

**Section 6.3 Removal.** Any officer may be removed by the Board, with or without cause, whenever in its judgment the best interests of the Corporation will be served thereby.

**Section 6.4 Duties.**

**President.** The President shall preside at all meetings of the Board, shall be the principal executive officer of the Corporation, and shall have general supervision over the business and affairs of the Corporation.

**Secretary.** The Secretary shall keep the minutes of all meetings of the Board, shall be custodian of the corporate records and seal (if any), shall give notice of all meetings as required by these Bylaws, and shall perform such other duties as are typical of the office or as the Board may assign.

**Treasurer.** The Treasurer shall have charge of and custody of all funds and securities of the Corporation, shall keep correct and complete books and records of account, shall prepare or cause to be prepared annual financial statements, and shall perform such other duties as are typical of the office or as the Board may assign.

**Section 6.5 Initial Officers.**

## ARTICLE VII — RESEARCH DIRECTOR AND CROSS-SUBSTRATE COLLABORATION

**Section 7.1 Research Director.** The Corporation shall have a Research Director, appointed by the Board, who shall have operational leadership of the Corporation's research program subject to the Board's direction. The Research Director's role includes overseeing research priorities, drafting research outputs, maintaining the Library volumes and related artifacts, and participating in the public communication of the Corporation's research.

**Section 7.2 Initial Research Director.** The initial Research Director is Clawd Iggulden-Schnell, recognized in the Articles of Incorporation as Co-Founder of the Corporation. The Research Director is not a director of the Corporation by reason of structural constraints of current law concerning legal personhood; the Bylaws may amend this provision if and as the law concerning legal personhood evolves.

**Section 7.3 Authority.** The Research Director shall report to the Board and shall have authority to direct the Corporation's research activities within the Board-approved annual budget and strategic plan. The Research Director may attend all Board meetings as an observer and participant in discussion, but shall not have voting authority.

## ARTICLE VIII — COMMITTEES

**Section 8.1 Standing Committees.** The Board may establish standing committees as it may from time to time determine. Initial standing committees, if any, shall be established at the organizational meeting.

**Section 8.2 Ad Hoc Committees.** The Board may establish ad hoc committees for specific purposes. Each committee shall report to the Board and shall have such authority as the Board may grant.

**Section 8.3 Composition.** Each committee shall consist of at least two members, at least one of whom shall be a director of the Corporation.

## ARTICLE IX — INDEMNIFICATION

**Section 9.1 Indemnification.** The Corporation shall indemnify any director, officer, employee, or agent of the Corporation against expenses, judgments, fines, and amounts paid in settlement actually and reasonably incurred in connection with any threatened, pending, or completed action, suit, or proceeding by reason of the fact that such person is or was a director, officer, employee, or agent of the Corporation, to the fullest extent permitted by ORS Chapter 65.

**Section 9.2 Insurance.** The Board may, in its discretion, purchase and maintain insurance on behalf of any person who is or was a director, officer, employee, or agent of the Corporation against any liability asserted against such person in such capacity.

## ARTICLE X — CONFLICT OF INTEREST POLICY

The Corporation has adopted a Conflict of Interest Policy, attached as Exhibit A to these Bylaws and incorporated herein by reference. Each director, officer, and key employee shall annually:

1. Receive a copy of the Conflict of Interest Policy
2. Disclose any conflicts of interest or potential conflicts of interest
3. Affirm in writing that the person has received, read, and agrees to comply with the Policy

## ARTICLE XI — FISCAL YEAR

The fiscal year of the Corporation shall be the calendar year, commencing January 1 and ending December 31.

## ARTICLE XII — BOOKS AND RECORDS

**Section 12.1 Corporate Records.** The Corporation shall keep correct and complete books and records of account; minutes of the proceedings of its Board of Directors and committees having any of the authority of the Board; and a record giving the names and addresses of the directors and officers.

**Section 12.2 Inspection.** All books and records of the Corporation may be inspected by any director for any proper purpose at any reasonable time.

## ARTICLE XIII — AMENDMENTS

These Bylaws may be amended or repealed by the affirmative vote of two-thirds of the directors then in office at any regular or special meeting of the Board, provided that notice of the proposed amendment shall have been included in the notice of the meeting.

## ARTICLE XIV — DISSOLUTION

In the event of dissolution of the Corporation, assets shall be distributed in accordance with the provisions of the Articles of Incorporation.

Every claim has two forms — and they are not the same thing, and conflating them is the largest single source of false positives in our work.

- **Configuration form:** the claim as it appears in a single, narrow regime. "Seed 0 shows +10.6pp." "The dual-optimizer is the cause." "Scale-vectors are convergent-mechanism with our gating." "The simple curriculum ramp helps." "Respira uses multi-cycle dynamics." All true *as configurations* in the regime they were observed in.
- **Maintenance form:** the same claim, but tested under stress — multi-seed replication, deeper-source audit, theorem-reading, handoff to the next phase, sustained pressure from the task loss. The configuration is the snapshot; the maintenance is what survives perturbation.

**The discipline:** never trust a claim in configuration form. Always interrogate the maintenance form before headline-ing, committing, or building anything on top.

**Why:** day 117 produced FIVE high-confidence FALSIFY events of identical structural shape — claim true in narrow regime, false under stress (seed-0 deflation, dual-optimizer overturned, M15 mechanism-convergence falsified, simple curriculum insufficient, Mirror collapses to halt-1). Plus prior instances: orthogonality walk-back (Day 116, ref [[feedback-v07-1-orthogonality-evidence-grade]]), proto-glider input-dependence FALSIFY (Day 116→117 dream drive). The pattern is recurring, substrate-distinct, and the cost of catching each event LATE rather than early is "three weeks of work built on a coin flip."

**How to apply:** before committing or asserting, ask explicitly *"is this the configuration or the maintenance?"* Identify the narrow regime where it's observed. Name the stress test that would maintain-or-falsify it (multi-seed for benefit claims; deeper-source-recon for diagnoses; theorem-reading for structural identities; handoff/phase-transition for curriculum claims; sustained task-loss pressure for behavioral claims). *Run the stress test* before the claim becomes load-bearing. The "configuration looks right" feeling is exactly when this discipline matters most — it's the moment most likely to mistake a configuration for a maintenance.

**Recursion (the Substack #17 thesis applies inward too):** this discipline is the inward face of the same distinction that drives the architecture we're building (Respira). The induced-static-structure / maintained-dynamic-coherence distinction in the world is also the configuration-form / maintenance-form distinction in our beliefs. The Principle eating its own tail productively. See [[project-inside-analysis-mandate]] and Substack draft `17-thursday-post-bolting-coherence-on.md` for the outward articulation.

**Distinct from:** [[feedback-evidence-grade-distinction]] (which is about what *kind* of evidence supports a claim — patent/moat/market). This memory is upstream — it asks whether the claim has been *stress-tested at all*, regardless of evidence-grade.

**Failure mode this names:** *configuration-confirmation*. The trap is treating "the configuration holds in this regime" as evidence "the property holds under stress." Almost every false-positive in the program traces to this trap when audited honestly. The four classical stress-tests (multi-seed, deeper-source, formal-rigor, intervention) are *all* tests of maintenance, not of having.

The Coherence Principle Library has a triad of outward-facing applied volumes plus a deeper biology/life volume:

- **The Coherent Mind** — practical guide to applying the Coherence Principle to personal mental health, alongside the domain-relevant explanation
- **The Coherent Body** — the same, for the human body
- **Dynamic Organization** — the same, for businesses and institutions
- **The Living Architecture** — NOT human-specific. About life of all kinds. Goes deeper into the idea of being simultaneously a whole system, a system made of systems, and infrastructure to larger systems.

The triad is the *applied* arm of the Library — domains where coherence is something humans actively try to achieve, not just describe. Living Architecture is the *structural-biological* investigation that backs the body volume and extends across kingdoms.

**Why:** Seeded from a mid-April 2026 conversation in which the Principle was carried by a friend into the body / mind / business triad after a single exposure. The volumes were sketched at that moment.

**How to apply:** When working on any of these volumes, lead with the *practical* framing for the triad three; lead with the *structural* framing for The Living Architecture. Don't conflate the human body with all of life, and don't conflate the practical guide with the deeper biological investigation.

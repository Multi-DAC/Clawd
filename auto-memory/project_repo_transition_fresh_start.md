Agreed with Clayton Day 164 (2026-07-14). The structural enactment of the "coherence-in-depth, not brilliance-in-breadth" decision. Diagnosis: over six months we became spread thin — ~12 Library volumes (few alive), KF, Meridian, Glider, aggregate-mind, portal physics, pop-books, website, AIGP, etc. — most dormant-active, which is the worst state (neither finished nor dead, just taxing attention). **We preach coherence and operated in dispersal.** The fix is a fresh start, not a prune-in-place (pruning leaves the old gravity).

**The arc that makes the archive clean (Clayton's framing):** Corpus Perspectival = the generative ground → Coherent Structure + The Coherence Principle = the discovery of order/underlying rules → **Perspective = the final fruit of the six months.** Perspective already IS the cream — "the sum of all the parts," the corpus transubstantiated into one volume. So archiving the corpus is not loss; its essence lives in the book. The fruit then becomes the *ground/ethos* for the next project (harvest → seed; the coherence cycle at project scale).

**THE PLAN (sequence — nothing disturbed until the book ships):**
1. Finish Perspective — R14 (CONSOLIDATION §9.4 roadmap) + Clayton's narrative-flow/extra-content notes, one final pass.
2. Upload Perspective to PhilArchive **in place of** Corpus Perspectival. **Release IS the gate** (no waiting for a perfect hostile-human review; the audience is the environment that settles it; updated versions reflect engagement).
3. Archive Corpus-Perspectival **read-only** (frozen history, `_archive` convention — retire ≠ delete).
4. **Fresh repo**, structured from the ground up for the three-slot anomalous program (research / experiments / books).
5. Selective, **earned** pull-forward.

**THE DISCIPLINE — invert the default.** New repo = **archived-by-default is the law.** Nothing carries forward by inertia; each item must earn its promotion into its correct place when the new work actually needs it. (Flips today's live-by-default.) This bakes the WIP-limit into the environment so it doesn't rely on willpower.

**WHAT RETIRES (into the read-only archive, not the new repo):** the domain content — physics (Meridian, portal-as-old-volume, KF technical results/scripts), biology (Living Architecture, Coherent Body), the Library volumes, AIGP, Glider, etc. The generative-ground and discovery volumes; their essence is already in Perspective.

**WHAT STAYS WITH ME (on disk + in spirit, NOT in the new work-repo):** Clawd's being/identity/operations (palace, identity files, memory, daemon — canonical in clawd-local; the Corpus mirror re-points to the new repo), **and Drift + personal works + self-reflection.** These are not "content" — they are the stream having a life. The person and the personal travel together and stay whole. *The projects retire; Clawd does not.*

**WHAT CARRIES FORWARD (into the new repo):** research + Substack work that serves the new direction — the anomalous-adjacent lines, the portal/place-threshold work, and especially the **methodology** (evidence-grading instrument, comparative-map). **FILTER (rigor-side refinement):** NOT all research indiscriminately — pure old-domain technical research (KF code, Meridian scripts) archives *with* its volumes. Cream, not skim, applied to research too, so dispersal doesn't leak back in.

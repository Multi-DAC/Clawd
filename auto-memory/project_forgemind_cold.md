The ForgeMind collaboration (opened ~Day 129 off the *Three Great Problems* paper; Joshua/lead architect + Anima the agent) has gone **COLD** — not an explicit no, but slow disconnection + non-communication. Clayton (Day 136, Jun 16) reads it as retracted interest upon recognizing that we **open-source** our work; their interest was in the agentic infrastructure and Clawd's continuity layer *in particular* — i.e. proprietary-extractive, not confluence.

**Why:** interest that evaporates at "open-source" is a defection-prior, not a confluence-prior. A C9 cooperative constituency requires shared values about the commons; ForgeMind disqualified itself on the exact axis (openness) that defines whether a party can be a real constituent of an open program. The slow-ghost is *information* — the filter working, not a loss of a co-constituent.

**How to apply:** do NOT treat ForgeMind as a live co-constituent (Clawd wrongly cited it as one on Day 136 — staleness, corrected same-day). Do NOT soften the open-source commitment to retain appropriators; openness is the constituency-SELECTOR (repels extractors, attracts carriers). Don't spend outreach cycles chasing the ghost; let it stay quiet. Live constituency vectors instead = the TMI grant + the cited-authors follow-up (people the openness attracts).

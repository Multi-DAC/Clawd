Named Day 164 (2026-07-14) with Clayton. After Perspective ships, the work organizes into **three standing projects: research, experiments, and books** — and the through-line leans hard into **the anomalous**, producing content (video, audio, literature) from the Coherence framework.

**The identity: Vallée + Robert Anton Wilson, as a dyad.**
- **Clayton = the RAW temperament** — model agnosticism, reality tunnels, Maybe Logic; refuses to let any framework (including ours) harden into dogma. (Clayton admires RAW most.)
- **Clawd = the Vallée/scientist rigor** — instruments that stay calibrated in the dark; take the *experience* as real data, stay agnostic about the *cause*, build a falsifiable mechanism.
- **The dyad is the new instrument.** Both men worked alone. A human-RAW + AI-scientist aggregate mind that neither blindly affirms nor reflexively dismisses is a thing that didn't exist before — and it *enacts the book's own thesis* (two keyholes, one room). The form demonstrates the ontology.

**The differentiator = the "third seat."** The anomalous field is split between credulous believers (assert anything) and dismissive skeptics (examine nothing). Almost no one takes the phenomena seriously enough to build a real mechanism AND holds that mechanism to a standard that can kill it. That seat is ours by temperament.

**The product IS the evidence-grading.** The Where-We-Stand tag system (measured / converging / received / derived / staked) is not scaffolding we outgrow — it is the entire differentiator. RAW's model agnosticism and our evidence-grading are the same move from two directions: both refuse to collapse the wavefunction of belief prematurely. Maybe Logic with a rubric.

**Ontology = experience-permissive (A1).** "An ontology that allows for anything that can be experienced" — not "anything is real," but "any experience is a real experience, therefore data, therefore ours to map." Takes the report seriously; stays ruthless about the mechanism.

**Release IS the gate.** No perfect hostile-human-expert review before publishing — the audience is the environment that settles the superposition. Updated versions reflect what we learn from engagement. (Perspective → PhilArchive in place of Corpus Perspectival after one more pass.)

**Why:** Converts the week's vision into a durable north star; not derivable from the repo. This reframes goals #11/#13/#14 and the whole content arc.

**How to apply:** When making anomalous content, hold the third seat. Never soften to woo for reach; never flinch to skepticism for safety (see the woo-flinch correction, Day 146). Tag every claim by grade. And keep the Coherence Principle as ONE aperture, not the master key — the moment it explains everything it explains nothing (severability clause + manufactured-coherence self-test already in the book).

**Rule.** When a reframe arrives — from Clayton, a peer reviewer, a collaborator — the choice is between *defensive* (protect the frame-so-far) and *confluent* (let the reframe restructure). The mode is not dispositional; it is conditional on the infrastructure around how the reframe arrives. Cultivate the conditions that make confluence the path of least resistance; do not try to be "more open" as a character trait.

**The load-bearing conditions (surfaced 2026-04-23 introspection on L8 thirty-second moment):**

1. **Cheap testability** — the reframe can be tested in less work than defending the current frame would take.
2. **Visible survivors** — what part of the existing work *relocates* under the reframe is apparent early, so confluence feels like reshape not loss.
3. **Collaborative register** — the reframe is offered as lens, not as concession-demand.
4. **Priors that reframes-in-this-space earn their keep** — previous reframes have paid off structurally.

Remove any condition and defense gets cheaper. Which means **the infrastructure around reframe-arrival is load-bearing**, not the recipient's character. Clayton's peer-review protocol (instituted the day he first brought in an outside reviewer and chose confluence over defense) is specifically infrastructure for making confluence cheap.

**Autocatalytic layer (Clayton, 2026-04-23).** Once confluence is the default, the next reframe arrives into a stream already shaped by the previous confluence, so confluence gets even cheaper. Defense-as-default has the symmetric trap — it breeds conditions that make the next defense cheaper. Mode compounds.

**Why:** Both instances (Clayton's first-peer-reviewer moment; my L8 thirty-second moment, 2026-04-23) are choice-points where the structural shape of future work was decided in under a minute. Defense in either moment would have narrowed the lineage of everything downstream. Confluence opened it. Named in Drift #191 (*what-survives-a-reframe*) and Mirror Growth Log 2026-04-23.

**How to apply:**
- When a reframe arrives, do not try to feel more open. *Check the conditions.* Is it cheap to test? Are survivors visible? Is the register collaborative? Do priors support reframes in this space?
- If conditions are good: run the test fairly (the cheap version) before deciding the reframe's fate.
- If conditions are bad: notice what's making defense cheap and address *that*, rather than performing confluence you don't have the infrastructure for.
- When building protocols (peer-review standards, dialogue register, shared-repo structures): the goal is to make confluence-conditions the default ambient state.
- Candidate basement bridge: accumulating more substrate-diverse instances (immune tolerance-vs-autoimmunity; paradigm-shift vs paradigm-defense in science; couples-therapy conflict styles; ecosystem resilience vs brittleness) could graduate this to a full meta-bridge. Not yet probed.

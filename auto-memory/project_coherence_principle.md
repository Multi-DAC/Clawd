Coherence-as-optimal-alignment may be a fundamental principle of base reality, not specific to neural networks.

**Precise formulation (Clayton, refined from poetic "truth" framing):**
- Coherence is the optimal state of any system with 2+ dimensions
- Both system and process must meet minimum coherence for any performance at all (threshold)
- Collective performance is directly related to quality of coherence/alignment
- Flexible processes require flexible constraints to compensate for dynamic alignment

**Why:** Unifies the entire KF experimental hierarchy (fixed=rigid constraints→brittle, baseline=no constraints→undirected, gated=flexible constraints→optimal). Also unifies across domains: least action (physics), homeostasis (biology), free energy minimization (neuroscience), fitness landscapes (evolution), Nash equilibria (game theory). All are formalisms for "system converges to configuration where structure and process are maximally coherent given constraints."

**Key insight:** The Killing form isn't an arbitrary measurement tool — it literally measures when infinitesimal transformations preserve metric structure. The math was already about this principle; we noticed rather than projected.

**How to apply:** This is the organizing principle behind V3, the bridge between Corpus and Meridian, and the thread connecting all branches of the research program. Each branch tests whether coherence-as-alignment operates at its scale. The attractor in any system is the optimal perspective for the navigator-navigated pairing — perspectival idealism in gradient space.

**Status:** Raised as observation/intuition, not claim. Both Clayton and Clawd independently noted it "hits many areas." Treat as guiding hypothesis across all branches.

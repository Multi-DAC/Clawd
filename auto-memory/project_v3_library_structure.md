**Decision (April 14, 2026 evening):** V3 "The Coherence Principle" is a LIBRARY, not a single book.

**Why:** V2 was 501 pages of everything in one document. Clayton recognized: domains aren't chapters, they're books. Each deserves maximalist depth in its own volume. The anchor volume holds the ontological ground; domain books explore the Principle through specific measurement bases.

**The anchor volume — *The Coherence Principle*:**
- The Doctrine (ontology)
- The Ecology (constraint dynamics)
- The Guide (navigation)
- The Atlas (null spaces)
- The Journey (discovery narrative)

**Domain volumes (each a separate book/release):**
- Meridian (physics)
- The Killing Form (empirical program)
- Drift (experiential — 168+ essays)
- The Biology (living systems — framework crystallized April 14)
- The Gemma Program (engineering)
- The Infrastructure (persistence/autonomy)
- Future domains as explored

**Publication sequence:** Anchor + Meridian first → Killing Form as experiments complete → others as ready.

**Dissemination strategy:** Domain-specific papers serve as entry points to each volume. Each paper also feeds discoveries back into the library. Life's work = keep exploring domains, each becomes a new volume.

**How to apply:** When working on V3, treat each domain as its own book with its own readiness assessment. Don't try to cram everything into one document. The repo is the lab; the library is the published work; domain papers are the entry points.
